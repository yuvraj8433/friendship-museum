Privacy Policy

Last updated:

("we", "us") lets you build a personalised digital gift for a friend. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Gift content you enter: your name, your friend's name, notes, photos, captions, message and friendship rules. We store this so the gift link can open for the person you send it to.
  • Replies: if your friend finishes the gift, we store their puzzle time, moves and any reply they choose to send.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive a payment ID and payment status.
  • Referral account: if you join the referral program we store your name, your email, your referral code, the referrals that came from it and your withdrawal history. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and we never see it. When someone you invite pays and creates a gift, we also record that person's name, the gift and the payment ID so that we can verify the referral. Friends you invite see only your first name.
  • Withdrawal details: the UPI ID you give us when you withdraw, kept only to pay you. It is visible only to you (when logged in) and to us.
  • Basic technical data: browser and device information that hosting providers log automatically.

How we use it

To create and deliver your gift link, show replies to you, process payment, prevent misuse and provide support. We do not sell your data and do not use your photos for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay (payments), Google Firebase (storing gifts and replies) and our website host. They process data only to provide their service.
  • Anyone who has your gift link can open that gift, so share the link only with the person it is for.

Retention and deletion

We keep a gift until you ask us to remove it. To delete a gift or your data, email with the gift link and we will remove it.

Children

The service is not intended for children under 13 creating gifts on their own.
