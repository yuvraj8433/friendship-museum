Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

Summary: your gift link is a digital product delivered instantly, so payments are generally non-refundable once the link is created. We will refund you in the cases below.

We will refund if

  • You were charged but no gift link was created.
  • You were charged twice for the same gift.
  • The link does not work and we cannot fix it within 3 working days.

We cannot refund if

  • You change your mind after the link was created and shared.
  • You entered wrong names, photos or text (we can help you fix a gift, just ask).

How to ask

Email within 7 days of payment with your payment ID and gift link. We reply within 2 working days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach you in 5-7 working days.

Cancellation

You can cancel any time before paying. There is no subscription and nothing renews.
